# Humor research for the doll's punch lines

Why this exists: the owner rejected the first batch as "too cynical" and asked for lines that are **שנון** in the
humor of "הפשוטע" and "קהילת הלימונים" (also naming Roy Kafri, Ido Katzir, "אסי וגורי", "הפרלמנט"). This is what
those sources actually do, and the recipe that `punchlines.he.md` follows.

## What was read (and what was not)

- **הפשוטע, spoken:** YouTube auto captions (Hebrew, rough but readable) of four episodes of the podcast "תרגעו"
  (Bar Almaliah + Shir Hadas Meir): ep. 10, 77, 86, 100, about 50 minutes read closely out of four hours pulled;
  the whole first episode of the TV series "הפשוטע" (Kan 11, 24 min) and a 6-minute Kan item about it.
- **הפשוטע, written about:** Hebrew Wikipedia, a ynet review, a Maariv item, the podcast's RSS episode notes (126).
- **קהילת הלימונים:** only video titles, descriptions and credits of the YouTube channel. Captions were refused
  (YouTube bot check), the Facebook group and Instagram need a login, and the Sapir review returned 403. Treat this
  section as thinner than the first.
- **Kafri / Katzir / אסי וגורי / הפרלמנט:** not researched in this pass; two lines each from general knowledge,
  no quotes, unverified.
- Auto captions mis-hear words. Quotes below are short, cleaned of caption noise, and marked (cc).

## הפשוטע

A parody persona (Facebook 2017, Instagram 2019): an eternal 22-year-old from the south, lazy, late, in love with
herself and with 2000s pop culture (telenovelas, פסטיגל, "השיר שלנו"). The ynet reviewer's summary is the best
one-line description of the voice: a "הפצצת שטיח של שיבושי שפה עממיים ורפרנסים לתרבות הפופ", delivered in a
register he calls "העילגת־ובעצם־שנונה" ([ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/yedioth/article/yokra14839800)).
It is never bitter: the character is delighted with herself while everything collapses around her.

Vocabulary and rhythm, as heard in the podcast: אחותי, אייקון / אייקוני, מעצים, נודר, טפו חמסה, כפרה, יאללה,
"לא להגזים", "מה נסגר", "תכבדי אותי"; feminine address to everyone; a Yiddish or Arabic word dropped in
straight-faced ("גורנישט"); brand and place names said in full (ארומה, איקאה, מודיעין, אופקים, קו 378).
Sentences are short and the funny word comes last. The owner's own "נודר" is this register exactly.

What to avoid, from the same sources: (1) the review's complaint, "הצורך הכפייתי להנחית עוד דימוי ועוד דימוי":
one image per line, never two; (2) the page was once taken down over a joke about an ethnic group. The persona
laughs at herself and at pop culture; a label like the owner's "ג׳וני דפ התימני" works only because the doll says
it about itself, with pride.

## קהילת הלימונים

A public Facebook group that describes itself as "קבוצת פייסבוק ציבורית מצחיקה אובייקטיבית"
([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@lemons_community95)): members post nonsense dubs and mash-ups. What the titles
show:

- **High and low glued together, deadpan.** A foreign clip carrying a very Israeli soundtrack, announced as a plain
  fact: "30 שניות למאדים - בדד", "מואנה - צוות שאני בונה". This is the same move as "ג׳וני דפ התימני".
- **Mock-official packaging of rubbish:** "אל תוותר - קליפ רישמי" (misspelling kept), "יום שישי.mp4",
  "הקרן והיחידה להכוונת חיילים משוחררים" as the title of an 18-second joke.
- **Flat understatement:** "פרפר לא נחמד", "אם אני אשמע (הגרסה הדו קוטבית)".
- **Stage names that are a joke by themselves:** credits like "צחי המפרום", "מוטי לוכים".

Lesson for the doll: say the absurd thing as a dry fact, with a label, and do not explain it.

## The other reference points (from memory, unverified)

- **Roy Kafri:** absurd premises played completely straight, musical nonsense, never winks.
- **Ido Katzir:** an Israeli type recognised from one phrase and one prop.
- **אסי וגורי:** characters with total, unearned self-confidence.
- **הפרלמנט ("מה קשור"):** café experts ruling with full authority on trivia.

All four share what the owner's three lines share: complete confidence, zero self-pity.

## The recipe: a שנון line for this doll

1. **Say who the doll has become, not what it is wearing.** A type, a job, or a celebrity moved to a small Israeli
   place. The accessory is never named in the line.
2. **Big title, small local detail, ego intact.** The status may collapse ("קפטן. של סירת פדלים בירקון."); the
   doll's opinion of itself never does. That is the difference between שנון and sad.
3. **One concrete proper noun carries the punch:** a place, a brand, an institution, a price (הירקון, איקאה,
   תמ״א 38, בורגר קינג, 2,990). Generic nouns are not funny.
4. **The funny word comes last. 3 to 12 words, full stops, no exclamation marks** except on a one-word reaction.
   One image per line.
5. **Bend something everyone knows** (a proverb, a customer-service sentence, a TV format, a board game) instead of
   a spelling pun. It has to work by ear, across a noisy room.
6. **Diva register:** the doll praises itself in the words of the feed (אייקון, נודר, טפו חמסה) and is happy.
   No rent, bank, ex, therapy, loneliness. Nothing about a group, nothing sexual, no politics.
7. **Shared memory of people aged 28 to 40:** the 2000s on TV, weddings and bar mitzvahs, the trip after the army,
   Eilat, IKEA, the family WhatsApp group, the building committee.
8. **The repeat test:** can a guest turn to a friend and repeat it as a nickname for the doll? If it needs a
   second sentence to explain it, cut it.

Words and constructions that carry the voice:

- "[שם מפורסם] מ[עיר קטנה]" / "[שם] ה[כינוי]"; "[תואר]. של [משהו קטן]."; "כמו X, רק ב־Y."
- "[תואר]. [פרט מנהלי]." ("מריו. אינסטלטור. מגיע בין שמונה לארבע.")
- נודר, צדיק, בן פורת יוסף, מחורחר, אחשילי, אייקון, טפו חמסה, יאללה, תכבדו, לא ניתפס לקטנות, מהדורת־, גרסת־.
- Use the "celebrity from a town" move at most once per accessory; it is the bullseye, and it wears out fast.
