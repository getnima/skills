# The doll's punch lines: voice guide

What the Shimi doll says in the dressing room when a guest puts an accessory on it.

The owner gave three lines of his own (marked (owner) below; they are the bullseye), rejected the first batch as
"too cynical", and asked for lines that are **שנון**: witty, sharp, a clever twist, quotable, in the humor of
"הפשוטע" and "קהילת הלימונים". The research behind this guide, with real examples, is in `humor-research.md`.
This file replaces the earlier guide and all of its lines.

## The voice

Shimi is a self-important diva and local legend who is delighted with himself. Whatever you put on him, he has
just become someone, and he is thrilled about it.

1. **Say who the doll has become, not what it is wearing.** A recognisable Israeli type, a job, or a celebrity
   moved to a small Israeli place ("ג׳וני דפ התימני"). Do not name the accessory.
2. **Big title, small local detail, ego intact.** The status may collapse; the doll's opinion of itself never does.
3. **One concrete proper noun is the punch:** a place, brand, institution or price.
4. **Form:** first person, one sentence or two very short ones, 3 to 12 words, the funny word last, full stops.
   An exclamation mark only on a one-word reaction. One image per line. No emoji, no "חחח".
5. **Bend something everyone knows** (a proverb, a service-desk sentence, a TV format). It must work by ear.
6. **Happy, never bitter.** No rent, bank, ex, therapy, loneliness, absent fathers. Nothing sexual, nothing about
   any group of people, no politics, no jokes on the guest.
7. **The repeat test:** would a guest repeat it to a friend as a nickname for the doll? If not, cut it.

The owner's exclamations belong to the voice: "נודר" (for real), "צדיק!", "בן פורת יוסף!" (nice), "וואו!",
"מחורחר!" (that's crazy), "אחשילי".

## Do / don't

**Do:** name a type, a place, a brand; let the doll enjoy itself; end on the funny word.
**Don't:** describe the accessory; make the doll poor, lonely or abandoned; explain the joke; stack two images.

Three rejected lines and their rewrites:

| Rejected (bitter, sad) | Why it failed | Rewrite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| האוצר קבור בגינה של ההורים. גם אני, רגשית. | Self-pity; the laugh is at a wound. | האוצר שלי: חניה בפלורנטין. לא מגלה איפה. |
| שפם של אבא שיצא לקנות סיגריות ב־94׳. | Dark family material. | אבא שלך, באלבום מ־84׳. |
| ירח דבש. לבד. היה מקסים. | Loneliness is the joke. | ירח דבש. עם עצמי. שדרגו לנו לסוויטה. |

The subject is often the same (treasure, moustache, honeymoon). What changes is that the doll is winning.

## New accessories

Every new accessory gets two or three lines by the rules above. The owner vetoes lines one by one, so each line
must stand alone. Use the "celebrity from a small town" move at most once per accessory.
