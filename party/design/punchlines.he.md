# The doll's punch lines: voice guide

What the Shimi doll says in the dressing room when a guest puts an accessory on it.
The owner asked for "super cynical and dense punch lines ... according to what the player chose", gave three
lines himself, and approved the batch below with "this is the comedic line in general".

## The voice

- First person, the doll talking about itself. Deadpan, self-aware, a little bitter, never cheerful.
- One sentence, or two very short ones with the punch in the second. Dense: no set-up words, no "haha", no emoji,
  no exclamation marks unless it is one loud word. 3 to 12 words is the sweet spot.
- Israeli everyday cynicism: rent, exits and "between jobs", weddings and the food boxes people fill at them,
  the big trip after the army, reserve duty, kibbutz nostalgia, Instagram courses, podcasts about productivity,
  IKEA, aunts with advice, the ex.
- The joke is always on the doll (a failed big shot who thinks he is a star), never on the guest and never on a group
  of people. No politics, no religion-bashing, nothing sexual, no illness jokes.
- Reference points the owner named for the register: the "קהילת הלימונים" and "הפשוטע" pages, Roy Kafri,
  Ido Katzir, "אסי וגורי", the "הפרלמנט" sketches ("מה קשור"). Write in that spirit with original lines; a light nod
  is fine, do not copy their jokes.
- The owner's own exclamations, usable as one-word reactions: "נודר" (for real), "צדיק!", "בן פורת יוסף!" (nice),
  "וואו!", "מחורחר!" (that's crazy), "אחשילי".

## Combination lines

The line should depend on what the guest chose, so some pairs get their own line that wins over the single-item
lines when both are worn. Write a dozen or more, for example: pirate hat + eye patch, crown + gold chain,
backwards cap + aviators, top hat + monocle, beer helmet + party hat, three hats in a tower, everything shaken off
(the naked doll), a full look in every slot.

## New accessories

Every new accessory gets two or three lines in this voice. The owner will veto individual lines, so keep each one
independent of the others.
